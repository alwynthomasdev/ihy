# Sample image sources

Every image in this folder is in the public domain or has been dedicated to it. All were downloaded from Wikimedia Commons, unmodified apart from the file name.

## beware.png
- **Content:** "Beware" (*I know a maiden fair to see*), a single-line melody in E♭ major, 3/4. Music score on p. 111 of *Tangles* by Margaret Cameron (Harper & Brothers, 1922).
- **Source:** https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:P_111--Tangles--Score--Beware_Beware.png
- **Original scan:** https://archive.org/details/tanglestalessom00brotgoog
- **Licence:** Public domain (published in the US in 1922).

## star-spangled-banner.png
- **Content:** "The Star Spangled Banner" (John Stafford Smith / Francis Scott Key), arranged for voice and piano in B♭ major, 3/4.
- **Source:** https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_Star_Spangled_Banner_Score,_With_Lyrics.png
- **Author:** Wikimedia Commons user Samvielsaa (own work, 2024).
- **Licence:** CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication). The underlying music and lyrics are also in the public domain.
